# Red Card

**You are the referee.** A first-person football game for Windows and macOS: you are on the
pitch, on foot, for a full 11-a-side match the game simulates for real. Run with play, look
down at your own boots, blow the whistle, point for the restart, show the card to the right
player — and find out from the assessor's report what really happened.

- **The game knows the truth.** Every tackle's outcome, every offside position, every ball
  over the line is recorded as it happens, and judged against the IFAB Laws of the Game.
- **Every decision a referee makes:** fouls, free kicks (direct and indirect), penalties,
  advantage, yellow and red cards, throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, offside with assistants
  who can be wrong, goals, dropped balls, the ball hitting you, substitutions, added time.
- **A career** from a village recreation ground to a cup final with VAR in your ear, marked
  out of ten by an assessor who replays your big decisions from your own eyes.
- **Training ground** (9 drills) and **challenges** (8 hard Laws: the last man, the dive,
  handball on the line, the wrong flag…).

## Controls
| | |
|---|---|
| WASD / left stick | move · Shift sprint |
| Mouse / right stick | look — look down to see your body |
| Left click | whistle (hold for the long whistle) |
| Right click | point: gives the restart the prompt shows |
| Space | advantage |
| Y / R | yellow / red card to the player you look at |
| X | wave the assistant's flag down |
| Q | arm up for an indirect free kick |
| B | dropped ball · U allow a substitution |
| Tab (hold) | your watch · N notebook · Esc pause |

Every key can be rebound in Settings; everything works on a gamepad.
